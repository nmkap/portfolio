Technology shapes how people work, connect, and experience the world. Whether it does that well is down to the people building it — and I want to belong to the team that gets it right.

Bridging the gap between research, design and code, I aim to create thoughtful digital experiences that feel like they were made for the person using them. To me, real design starts long before the first wireframe — in the research, the conversations, and the friction points most people overlook.

I want to work on products where the answer to "does this make someone's life better?" is always yes.
